#+title: Colorful Pele examples

Examples from [[https://pele.run/examples][the official Pele gallery]] by kepano and the Pele contributors.
Source diagrams are reproduced under the MIT license; see [[file:../docs/pele-examples-LICENSE.txt][the upstream license]].

Execute a block with =C-c C-c=, then =C-c C-x C-v= to display its SVG.
The checked-in previews below are rendered with this package.

* Hawaiʻi mindmap

Source: [[https://pele.run/examples/mindmap][Pele mindmap examples]].

#+begin_src pele :file ../docs/example-hawaii.svg
mindmap
  root((Hawaiʻi))
    History
      Old legends
      ::icon(fa fa-book)
      Voyaging
        Polynesian navigators who sailed by the stars
    Nature
      Mountains<br/>and volcanoes
      Ocean life
        Reef
            Sea turtles
            Monk seals
            Parrotfish
    Culture
      Hula and lei
      ʻUkulele
#+end_src

* Restaurant comparison

Source: [[https://pele.run/examples/radar][Pele radar examples]].

#+begin_src pele :file ../docs/example-restaurants.svg
radar-beta
  title Restaurant Comparison
  axis food["Food Quality"], service["Service"], price["Price"]
  axis ambiance["Ambiance"]

  curve a["Restaurant A"]{4, 3, 2, 4}
  curve b["Restaurant B"]{3, 4, 3, 3}
  curve c["Restaurant C"]{2, 3, 4, 2}
  curve d["Restaurant D"]{2, 2, 4, 3}

  graticule polygon
  max 5
#+end_src
